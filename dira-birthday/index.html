A few words from my heart

Dear Dira,
this is for you.

Some feelings are too meaningful to leave unsaid. Today, I wanted to give these words a little place of their own.

A LETTER
FOR DIRANOVEMBER 27 · WITH LOVE

There are some people who enter our lives and become more than just people we know. They become a beautiful part of our story. To me, you are one of those people.

Over the past five years, I have had the privilege of knowing you, talking to you, sharing moments with you, and discovering the beautiful person you are.

I admire your kind heart, the way you care about me in the ways you can, the love you have for your family, and your efforts to understand me even when life is not always easy for you.

I admire your love for God, your passion for learning, your curiosity, and your desire to keep discovering yourself.

Dira, I want you to know something: I truly love you. Not because I think you are perfect, but because there is something about you that makes you special to my heart.

I do not know everything the future holds, but one thing I know is that I would love to build something beautiful with you. I hope that, if God leads us there and we both choose it wholeheartedly, one day I may have the privilege of being your husband, loving you, respecting you, praying with you, and building a peaceful home together.

I am not giving you this message to put pressure on your heart or demand an answer. I simply want you to know how much you mean to me.

On your birthday, my greatest wish is that you grow closer to God, become everything He created you to be, achieve your dreams, and never lose the beautiful heart that makes you who you are.

Thank you for being Dira.

Happy Birthday, My Special One.
With love, prayers, and sincere wishes.

“Ndagukunda by'ukuri.”♡

God's word for you

May His word
live richly in you.

Two passages I hope will encourage your heart as you step into another year of life.

✦01 / A HEART FILLED WITH HIS WORD

Colossians 3:16

ENGLISH · KING JAMES VERSION

“Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly in all wisdom; teaching and admonishing one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your hearts to the Lord.”

IKINYARWANDA · BIBILIYA YERA — AMAGAMBO Y'UMURONGO

“Ijambo rya Kristo ribe muri mwe rigwiriye rifite ubwenge bwose, mwigishanye, muhugurane muri zaburi n’indirimbo n’ibihimbano by’umwuka, muririmbirirana Imana ishimwe mu mitima yanyu.”

May God's word fill your heart, shape your thoughts, and stay close to you through prayer, learning, and worship.

✧02 / TRUSTING HIS DIRECTION

Proverbs 3:5–6

ENGLISH · KING JAMES VERSION

“Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.”

IKINYARWANDA · IBISOBANURO (SI AMAGAMBO Y'UMURONGO UKO YAKABAYE)

Jya wiringira Uwiteka n'umutima wawe wose; ntukishingikirize ku bitekerezo byawe gusa. Mu nzira zawe zose ujye umwizera, na we azakuyobora.

May you feel free to trust God with the things you understand and the things you are still figuring out. He can guide every step.

Kinyarwanda wording for Colossians 3:16 follows Bibiliya Yera. The Proverbs 3:5–6 paraphrase is informed by Bibiliya Yera, but is not a direct quotation. English quotations are from the public-domain King James Version.

A prayer for your new year

A prayer
for Dira.

Dear Heavenly Father,

Dear Dira, I thank God for the gift of your life, for bringing you this far, and for all the beautiful things He is doing in your life.

May the Lord guide your steps, protect your heart, bless your family, and give you wisdom in everything you do.

May your love for God continue to grow. May you never lose your desire to pray, learn, grow, and become the woman God created you to be.

May God bless the dreams you carry in your heart. May He give you strength when you feel weak, peace when life becomes difficult, and courage to keep moving forward.

May your future be filled with His grace, purpose, and endless blessings.

In Jesus' name, Amen.
